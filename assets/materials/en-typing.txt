Practice makes perfect, but only deliberate practice makes progress. Sit up straight, keep your
wrists level, and rest your fingers on the home row before you start. Accuracy comes first, and
speed is what accuracy turns into once the movement becomes automatic. When you feel tension in
your hands, stop and shake them out, then begin again a little slower than before.

Keep your eyes on the screen, not on your hands. If you catch yourself looking down, that is the
signal to slow down rather than to try harder. Short sessions every day beat one long session a
week, because the point is not to finish the text but to teach your fingers where the keys live.
